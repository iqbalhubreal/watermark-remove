Features

Precise selection, two ways to clean up, any browser tab.

Everything you need for logos, captions and date stamps, without sending a file anywhere.

Remove

Fills the marked area from the pixels around it, working from the edges inward, then smooths the seams. Suited to plain and softly graded backgrounds.

Blur

Softens the selected shape until the mark is unreadable. It works on any background, including busy textures where Remove struggles.

Rectangle, Brush and Erase

Draw a box, paint over awkward shapes, then erase what you over-selected. The brush is sized on screen, shows its outline, and has keyboard shortcuts.

Images and video

Export images as PNG and videos as WebM with every frame and its original timing. Audio is kept when the file has a readable audio track. You can cancel a video export at any time.

Private by design

Your media is read and processed in browser memory. There are no uploads, accounts, analytics or third-party requests.

Fast, with a safety net

A compact WebAssembly kernel does the fill, and the selection is cached between video frames. If WebAssembly is unavailable, a JavaScript version takes over.

Under the hood

How Remove fills the gap

The selection is first filled with a smooth estimate that spreads colour inward from the pixels around it. That estimate is then rebuilt from real patches: for every small block inside the gap, the closest-matching block from the surrounding picture is found and copied in, working from a coarse scale down to full size. Grass stays grassy, bricks keep their lines and grain stays grainy.

On video, each frame starts from the previous frame's match and is blended with it while the scene is steady, so the filled area does not shimmer. The same steps run in a small WebAssembly kernel for speed, with a JavaScript fallback that gives identical pixels. It only reuses what is already in the picture, so it is a patch-based fill, not generative AI.

Privacy

Your files stay in your browser.

The studio is a set of static files. It has no server component that could receive your media.

  • Media is read with browser file APIs and drawn to a canvas in memory.
  • No analytics, trackers, cookies or accounts.
  • Scripts and fonts ship with the site, so there are no third-party requests.
  • The only thing stored is your light or dark theme choice, in your own browser.

Check it yourself

You do not have to take our word for it.

  1. Press F12 to open developer tools and select the Network tab.
  2. Open an image or video in the studio and process it.
  3. Read the request list. It contains only this site's own files, never your media.
  4. For a stricter test, turn your network off after the page loads. The studio keeps working.
Results

What to expect from Remove.

Remove reuses the colours around your selection, so the background decides how good the result looks.

Works well on

  • Logos, text and date stamps on plain or softly graded backgrounds
  • Skies, walls, studio backdrops and out-of-focus areas
  • Small and medium selections that follow the shape of the mark

Can show artifacts on

  • Fine textures, patterns, hair and foliage
  • Large selections or marks that cover faces and small details
  • Video with a textured background, where each frame is filled on its own

Tips: select tightly with the brush and trim with Erase, raise edge softness to 2 to 4 px, use fewer refinement passes to keep detail, and switch to Blur if the fill looks smeared.

FAQ

Common questions

Is my image or video uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is opened and processed inside your browser tab, and the result is saved straight from it. The site loads no external scripts, fonts or analytics.

You can confirm this in your browser's Network tab, as described in the privacy section.

Which file types work?

Images in any format your browser can open, such as JPEG, PNG and WebP, export as PNG. Video in formats your browser can play, most reliably H.264 MP4 and WebM, exports as WebM.

How is video exported, and why does it take a while?

Every frame of the original is processed once, encoded with its exact timestamp and packed into a WebM file, so the result keeps the source's frame count and timing and plays smoothly. This is slower than playing the video through, because a frame is only taken when the previous one is finished, and larger selections take longer. Keep the tab visible; if you switch away, the export pauses and continues when you return. You can cancel at any time.

Will the exported video have sound?

Audio is included when your browser exposes the audio track of the source video. If it does not, the export is silent and the studio tells you so when it finishes.

Is this AI generative fill?

No. Remove is a patch-based fill that copies matching texture from around your selection. It works well on plain and repetitive backgrounds and keeps texture, but it cannot invent objects or detail that are not in the picture. Smaller, accurate selections look best.

How large a file can I use?

It depends on your device. Everything is held in memory, so very large images and long high-resolution videos need more memory and time. Smaller, accurate selections are also faster.

Which browsers are supported?

Current versions of Chrome and Edge are the best tested. Images should work in any modern browser. Frame-accurate video export needs a browser with WebCodecs (recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox); other browsers fall back to a real-time recording.

Can I host it myself or use it offline?

Yes. The site is plain static files with no build step. Upload the folder to any web host, or open index.html from your disk.

Use it on media you have the right to edit.

Watermarks often mark ownership. Use this tool for your own photos and videos, content you are licensed to modify, or files where the owner has given permission. Removing a watermark from someone else's work without permission can infringe their rights.
